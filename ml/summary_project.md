**CreditPulse — AI-Powered SME Credit Health Assessment**

CreditPulse addresses a core gap in SME lending: banks currently assess creditworthiness using narrow, slow, and often subjective criteria — typically limited to GST filings and self-reported financials — leaving both lenders and small business owners without a fast, reliable picture of actual credit health.

Our platform consolidates the same signals a real bank underwriter evaluates — CIBIL score, GST compliance, ITR history, balance sheets, banking conduct, business age, ownership structure, location, competitive positioning, and digital reputation — into 13 deterministic, individually-computed factor scores. These scores are then aggregated not by a hand-picked formula, but by a **trained machine learning model** (logistic regression, validated at 87.4% held-out accuracy) that learned real interaction effects between risk factors — for instance, that a single weak signal matters far less than multiple weak signals compounding together.

The result is a single, explainable Bank Confidence Score, generated instantly and entirely client-side, with a transparent breakdown any lender or business owner can inspect and trust — closing the gap between complex underwriting data and a decision anyone can actually understand.
